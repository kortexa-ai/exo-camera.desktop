# Exo Camera 0.3.2 — Early Alpha

This release makes **Exo Camera Microphone** available in WhatsApp for Mac.

## Download and install

Download **ExoCamera-0.3.2.pkg**, open it, and follow macOS Installer. Open Exo Camera from
Applications. If you already use the microphone, choose **Update** for it in the menu panel and
enter your password. Otherwise choose **Install** for the camera and, optionally, the microphone.
Select **Exo Camera** and **Exo Camera Microphone** in your call app.

Requirements: macOS 14+, with Apple Silicon recommended. Runtime acceptance on Intel is not yet complete.

## Fixed

- **WhatsApp lists Exo Camera Microphone.** WhatsApp for Mac hides every microphone that macOS
  reports as a virtual device. The microphone driver (0.2.1) now reports a hardware-style
  connection type, so WhatsApp lists it. Other apps list it as before. WhatsApp can also show
  Exo Camera Microphone as a speaker; keep your normal speakers selected there.

## Known limitations

See the [0.2.0 notes](0.2.0.md) for the alpha limitations that still apply: use Exo Camera's own
**Mute** to hide captions, one caption language at a time, gesture sensitivity to lighting,
experimental Codex login, physical inputs only, and no automatic updater.

## Privacy and support

Local models process on your Mac; enabling OpenAI features sends microphone audio to OpenAI.
Exo Camera does not record raw media.
[Report issues](https://github.com/kortexa-ai/exo-camera.desktop/issues) with your macOS version, Mac model,
app version, and a brief reproduction. See SHA256SUMS for the package hashes and NOTICE for attribution.
