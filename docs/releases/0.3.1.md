# Exo Camera 0.3.1 — Early Alpha

This release adds uninstallers, so you can remove Exo Camera, and the old AI Camera, without
Terminal. The app works the same as in 0.3.0.

## Download and install

Download **ExoCamera-0.3.1.pkg**, open it, and follow macOS Installer. Open Exo Camera from
Applications, then choose **Install** for the camera and, optionally, the microphone in the menu
panel. Approve the camera under **System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions** when macOS
asks. Select **Exo Camera** and **Exo Camera Microphone** in your call app.

Requirements: macOS 14+, with Apple Silicon recommended. Runtime acceptance on Intel is not yet complete.

## New

- **Uninstall-ExoCamera-0.3.1.pkg** removes Exo Camera. First remove its camera and microphone in
  **Settings → General → Virtual Devices**, then open the package and enter your password.
- **Uninstall-AICamera-0.3.1.pkg** removes the old AI Camera app the same way, after you remove AI
  Camera's devices in AI Camera.
- The [installation guide](https://github.com/kortexa-ai/exo-camera.desktop/blob/v0.3.1/docs/installation.md)
  now uses only normal Mac steps.

Both uninstallers stop without changing anything if the camera is still registered. They keep your
settings and downloaded models.

## Coming from AI Camera

Exo Camera installs beside AI Camera. On first launch it moves your AI Camera settings, notes,
Codex login, and downloaded models. macOS may ask once to let Exo Camera read a saved API key.
Then remove AI Camera's camera and microphone in AI Camera, quit it, and open
**Uninstall-AICamera-0.3.1.pkg**.

## Known limitations

See the [0.2.0 notes](0.2.0.md) for the alpha limitations that still apply: use Exo Camera's own
**Mute** to hide captions, one caption language at a time, gesture sensitivity to lighting,
experimental Codex login, physical inputs only, and no automatic updater.

## Privacy and support

Local models process on your Mac; enabling OpenAI features sends microphone audio to OpenAI.
Exo Camera does not record raw media.
[Report issues](https://github.com/kortexa-ai/exo-camera.desktop/issues) with your macOS version, Mac model,
app version, and a brief reproduction. See SHA256SUMS for the package hashes and NOTICE for attribution.
