# Exo Camera 0.3.0 — Early Alpha

AI Camera is now **Exo Camera**. Its new home is [exo.camera](https://exo.camera).

## Download and install

Download **ExoCamera-0.3.0.pkg**, open it, and follow macOS Installer. Open Exo Camera from
Applications, then choose **Install** for the camera and, optionally, the microphone in the menu
panel. Approve the camera under **System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Media
Extensions** when macOS asks. Select **Exo Camera** and **Exo Camera Microphone** in your call app.

Requirements: macOS 14+, with Apple Silicon recommended. Runtime acceptance on Intel is not yet complete.

## Coming from AI Camera

Exo Camera is a new app identity, with a new virtual camera and microphone. It does not replace
AI Camera in place. On first launch it moves your AI Camera settings, notes, Codex login, and
downloaded models to Exo Camera. macOS may ask once to let Exo Camera read a saved API key.

Then remove the old app, preferably before installing the new devices:

1. In AI Camera, open **Settings → General → Virtual Devices** and choose **Remove** for the camera
   and microphone. Turn off **Open AI Camera at login**, then quit AI Camera.
2. Remove `/Applications/AI Camera.app` as described in the
   [AI Camera removal steps](https://github.com/kortexa-ai/exo-camera.desktop/blob/v0.2.1/docs/installation.md#normal-removal).
3. Reselect **Exo Camera** and **Exo Camera Microphone** in your call apps.

## New

- **Layout and Mirror quick controls.** A 4:3 layout and an overlay mirror in the menu-bar toolbar
  for call apps that crop or flip the camera, such as WhatsApp.

## Known limitations

See the [0.2.0 notes](0.2.0.md) for the alpha limitations that still apply: use Exo Camera's own
**Mute** to hide captions, one caption language at a time, gesture sensitivity to lighting,
experimental Codex login, physical inputs only, and no automatic updater.

## Privacy, removal, and support

Local models process on your Mac; enabling OpenAI features sends microphone audio to OpenAI.
Exo Camera does not record raw media.
