Uninstall AI Camera

This package removes AI Camera from Applications, along with its microphone driver and installation records. macOS asks for your password because the app is protected.

Before you continue:

  1. Open AI Camera. In Settings → General → Virtual Devices, choose Remove for the camera and the microphone.
  2. If AI Camera says the camera removal needs a restart, restart your Mac first.
  3. Turn off Open AI Camera at login.

If the camera is still registered, the uninstaller stops without changing anything. Only AI Camera can remove its own camera.

Click Install to run the removal. Your settings and downloaded models are kept.
