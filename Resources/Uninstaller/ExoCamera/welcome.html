Uninstall Exo Camera

This package removes Exo Camera from Applications, along with its microphone driver and installation records. macOS asks for your password because the app is protected.

Before you continue:

  1. Open Exo Camera. In Settings → General → Virtual Devices, choose Remove for the camera and the microphone.
  2. If Exo Camera says the camera removal needs a restart, restart your Mac first.
  3. Turn off Open Exo Camera at login.

If the camera is still registered, the uninstaller stops without changing anything. Only Exo Camera can remove its own camera.

Click Install to run the removal. Your settings and downloaded models are kept.
