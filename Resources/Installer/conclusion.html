Exo Camera is in Applications.

Open Exo Camera, then click its camera icon in the menu bar.

  1. Install the virtual camera and optional microphone from the app.
  2. Follow macOS permission and Media Extension prompts.
  3. Select Exo Camera and Exo Camera Microphone in your call app.

Upgrading? Existing devices are kept. Update a system component separately only when you are ready; macOS may require approval or a restart for that operation.
