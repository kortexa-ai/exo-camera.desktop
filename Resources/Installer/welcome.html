A little intelligence in your camera.

Exo Camera adds live captions, translation, gestures, and a voice agent to your camera feed.

This installer places Exo Camera in Applications, or upgrades an existing copy. Your settings, downloaded models, and login are kept.

Camera and microphone setup happens separately inside the app. This installer does not install, update, or remove those system components.

Coming from AI Camera? Exo Camera is its new name. It installs beside AI Camera and moves your AI Camera settings and models on first launch; then remove AI Camera's devices in AI Camera and open the Uninstall-AICamera package from the release to remove the old app.

0.3.2 is an early alpha. Expect rough edges. To remove Exo Camera later, use the Uninstall-ExoCamera package from the release.
